FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can benefit from your OHS training?
Our training is designed for government institutions, private organizations, NGOs, and any workplace that wants to improve worker safety. We customize programs for all industries and staff levels.
Do you offer on-site training?
Yes. We come to your location and deliver training in your actual work environment, which makes the learning more practical and effective for your team.
How long does a typical training program take?
Programs range from half-day workshops to multi-day certification courses, depending on your needs. We'll work with you to design a schedule that fits your operations.
Do you provide certification?
Yes. Participants who complete our training programs receive certificates of completion that demonstrate their understanding of occupational health and safety principles.
What areas of sanitation do you consult on?
We advise on environmental sanitation, waste management, water safety, hygiene protocols, and regulatory compliance for public and private institutions.
